# NASA Data Sources & Academic Citations
# OUTPOST: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

This document catalogues all official NASA data repositories, academic publications, technical standards, and mission telemetry models utilized to calibrate **OUTPOST: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer** for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

---

## 1. Primary NASA Technical References & Datasets

### 1. NASA ECLSS Exploration Water Recovery Architecture
- **Source**: NASA Marshall Space Flight Center & Johnson Space Center
- **Focus**: Closed-loop water processing, urine distillation, catalytic oxidation, and 98% loop closure benchmarks.
- **Reference Document**: Carter, D. L., et al. (2023). *Exploration Water Recovery System Architecture and Ground Testing Milestones*. 52nd International Conference on Environmental Systems (ICES-2023-142).
- **URL**: [https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/space-station-research-and-technology/life-support-systems/](https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/space-station-research-and-technology/life-support-systems/)
- **License**: Public Domain / NASA Scientific Open Access

### 2. Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment (MOXIE)
- **Source**: NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) & Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
- **Focus**: High-temperature Solid Oxide Electrolysis Cells (SOEC) splitting atmospheric $CO_2$ into $O_2$ at $800^\circ\text{C}$ on Mars.
- **Reference Document**: Hecht, M., et al. (2021). *Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment (MOXIE)*. Space Science Reviews, 217(1), 9.
- **URL**: [https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020/spacecraft/instruments/moxie/](https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020/spacecraft/instruments/moxie/)
- **License**: NASA / JPL Open Public Mission Telemetry

### 3. Mars Science Laboratory Curiosity Rover: Radiation Assessment Detector (RAD)
- **Source**: NASA / Southwest Research Institute / Mars Science Laboratory
- **Focus**: Surface galactic cosmic ray (GCR) and solar particle event (SPE) dose equivalent rates on Mars.
- **Reference Document**: Hassler, D. M., et al. (2014). *Mars’ Surface Radiation Environment Measured with the Mars Science Laboratory’s Curiosity Rover*. Science, 343(6169), 1244797.
- **URL**: [https://science.nasa.gov/mission/msl-curiosity/instruments/rad/](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/msl-curiosity/instruments/rad/)
- **License**: NASA Planetary Data System (PDS)

### 4. Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) Diviner & CRaTER Instruments
- **Source**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center
- **Focus**: Lunar surface thermal cycling (+120°C to -130°C) and cosmic ray interactions with lunar regolith.
- **Reference Document**: Spence, H. E., et al. (2010). *CRaTER: The Cosmic Ray Telescope for the Effects of Radiation on the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter*. Space Science Reviews, 150, 243–284.
- **URL**: [https://lunar.gsfc.nasa.gov/](https://lunar.gsfc.nasa.gov/)
- **License**: NASA Open Science Data Repository

### 5. NASA Veggie & Advanced Plant Habitat (APH)
- **Source**: NASA Kennedy Space Center
- **Focus**: Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) spectrum tuning, transpiration water recovery, and nutrient stability of microgreens in space.
- **Reference Document**: Massa, G. D., et al. (2017). *Selection of leafy green crops for a space station plant food production facility*. Advances in Space Research, 60(11), 2412-2423.
- **URL**: [https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/growing-plants-in-space/](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/iss-research/growing-plants-in-space/)
- **License**: NASA Open Access Scientific Publications

### 6. NASA Systems Engineering Handbook (NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev 2)
- **Source**: NASA Headquarters, Washington, D.C.
- **Focus**: Dual-fault tolerance, trade study methodologies, subsystem coupling, and risk mitigation matrices.
- **Reference Document**: National Aeronautics and Space Administration. (2016). *NASA Systems Engineering Handbook (SP-2016-6105 Rev 2)*.
- **URL**: [https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)
- **License**: US Government Work / Public Domain
